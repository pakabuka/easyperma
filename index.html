Find your key in Perma settings. Folders and subfolders load when you paste your key. Use Refresh to reload them, or choose “Enter a folder ID manually.”

Files stay in your browser. URLs are sent to Perma. Content comparison reads the source and its saved archive without sending your API key to the source site.

Verified means the saved capture succeeded and its readable content substantially matches the source. Matching ignores navigation, scripts, and repeated page furniture. Pages that cannot be compared remain red until reviewed. Mark a saved page Usable to include it in Word.

Existing Perma links are kept as supplied and excluded from creation. Remove affects this list only. Word export inserts automatically verified links and new links you mark Usable beside their original footnote URLs as URL [https://perma.cc/XXXX-XXXX]. Failed, pending, and unapproved links stay unchanged.
