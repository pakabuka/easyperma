Finding your API Key

  1. Log in to perma.cc.
  2. Click your Name in the top right corner.
  3. Select Tools from the dropdown menu.
  4. Locate the "API Key" section on that page.

Privacy Policy

No Data Collection: This website operates entirely client-side (in your browser). We do not collect, store, or transmit your data to any third-party servers other than the official Perma.cc API when you execute a request.

Your API Key is used strictly to authenticate with Perma.cc and is not saved by this tool unless you use your browser's password manager.
